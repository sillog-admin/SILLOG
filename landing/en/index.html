Frequently asked questions

SILLOG is a macOS app that records what you do on your Mac and organizes it by task. Without keeping a work log, the apps you used, the documents and web pages you viewed, and your working time gather under each task. From those records, you can write weekly reports, retrospectives, and portfolio entries in AI chat.

Apps, window titles, URLs, file paths, on-screen text, screenshots, downloaded files, and the messages you type into AI coding tools (Claude Code, Codex CLI). If there is no input for more than 2 minutes, you are recorded as away. Audio is recorded only when you start a recording, and it is transcribed on your Mac.

On your Mac. SILLOG has no server of its own.

To organize your work and answer in chat, SILLOG sends record content to the AI account you connect, along with screenshots of key screens and recording transcripts. Audio files are never sent. Text taken from your records goes out with phone numbers, emails, card numbers, ID numbers, and API keys masked. Materials looked up in chat, web searches you allow, and connector traffic also leave your Mac. You can check what was sent in the activity log and turn sending off feature by feature.

Connect either a ChatGPT subscription or an OpenAI-compatible API. To chat with a ChatGPT subscription, the Codex CLI or the Codex app must be installed. Connecting through an API incurs usage fees.

Add it to the apps not to record, and only its name and time are kept. Password managers and Keychain are excluded from the start, and password fields are never recorded.
